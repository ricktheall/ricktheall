# User Test Script — TheAll Bible Coffee, Ephesians 1

**Do not run this test until `docs/MVP_STATUS.md` shows the content blockers cleared.**
Testing a draft chapter teaches you about the draft, not about the product.

Target: 5–10 readers. Ordinary Thai Christians, not designers, not pastors on staff,
not people who owe you a favour.

---

## Before the session

- Have the deployed URL ready as a plain link. Nothing else.
- Ask the participant to use **their own phone**, on their own connection.
- Have a way to note observations without staring at them — paper is fine.
- Decide in advance that you will not defend the design. Not once.

---

## Running the session

1. **Give the participant the link without explaining how the interface works.**
   No tour, no "so what you do is…". Hand it over and stop talking.

2. **Ask them to use their own phone.** Not your device, not a laptop.

3. **Do not teach or guide** unless they are completely blocked — meaning they have
   stopped and cannot find any way forward at all. If you must help, write down that
   you helped, and what you had to say.

4. **Observe and note** where they:
   - pause
   - hesitate before tapping
   - scroll quickly past a section
   - scroll backward to re-read
   - look confused, frown, or sigh
   - stop reading and check something else

5. **Record whether they reach the checkpoint** (the 3 Words Check at the end of the
   chapter). Yes or no. This is the primary behavioural signal.

6. **Record approximate total reading time**, from opening the link to finishing.

7. **Let them complete the three in-app feedback questions** on their own. Do not
   read the questions aloud, and do not watch over their shoulder as they type.

8. **Ask this final verbal question**, in these words:

   > “ถ้าไม่มีใครเตือน คุณจะกลับมาอ่านบทที่ 2 เองไหม เพราะอะไร?”

   Write down the *reason*, not just the yes or no. The reason is the finding.

9. **Ask what truth from Ephesians 1 they remember**, in their own words. Do not
   prompt with "chosen", "inheritance", or "deposit". If they can only repeat the
   checkpoint words back, that is a comprehension failure, not a success.

10. **Record observations without defending or explaining the design.** If they
    misread something, that is data. Write it down and move on.

## Collecting the in-app feedback

At the end of the flow the participant can tap **คัดลอกผลตอบกลับ**, which copies an
anonymous summary. Ask them to send it to you over LINE or any messaging app.

The summary contains no name, no email, no device identifier, and never their
reflection text. If a participant would rather not send it, that is fine — their
verbal answers are the more valuable data anyway.

---

## Reading the results

These are directional learning signals, not statistical proof.

| Testers | Continue signal |
| --- | --- |
| 5 | ≥ 4 reach the final checkpoint, and ≥ 3 choose “อยากอ่านแน่นอน” |
| 10 | ≥ 8 reach the final checkpoint, and ≥ 6 choose “อยากอ่านแน่นอน” |

All of the following must also hold:

- No repeated critical mobile-usability problem.
- Readers can explain the central truth in their own words.
- Feedback shows they valued **the content**, not merely the visual design.

Praise for how it looks, with no memory of what Ephesians 1 said, is a negative
result no matter how warm the compliments are.

### The questions that actually matter

- What did they understand?
- Where did they hesitate?
- What moved them?
- What confused them?
- What nearly made them stop?
- Would they return without being reminded?

---

## After testing

Do not begin building the production platform during this round. Write up what you
learned first, then decide.
